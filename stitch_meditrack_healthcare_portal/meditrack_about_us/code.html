The Challenge

Healthcare appointment processes and patient-facing information can sometimes be fragmented across paper records, phone calls, and disconnected systems. Patients often encounter friction when trying to remember clinic instructions, manage conflicting schedules, or verify their past visits.

The MediTrack Vision

MediTrack aims to explore how a centralised digital experience can help patients manage appointment information more conveniently. By focusing on intuitive design, accessibility standards, and calm communication, we are building tools that reduce administrative stress for both patients and healthcare providers.

Guiding Principles

The core commitments informing every design and technical decision we make.

favorite

Patient First

Every workflow, button, and notification is crafted around the emotional state and practical needs of patients during their care journey.

Empathy-led UI Priority 01
shield_lock

Privacy by Design

We treat personal data with strict restraint, prioritising minimised collection, role-based boundaries, and full transparency.

Zero Unnecessary Logs Priority 02
accessibility_new

Accessibility for Everyone

Healthcare is a universal necessity. We design to strict WCAG 2.2 AA standards so people of all visual, physical, and cognitive abilities can participate.

WCAG 2.2 AA Baseline Priority 03

Continuous Improvement

We actively solicit feedback from patients, clinicians, and administrative staff to iteratively refine usability and remove barriers.
